        <p class="about-tile__number">01 / An artsy-fartsy beginning</p>
        <h3>I started with stories and people.</h3>
        <p>
          I was the artsy-fartsy kid who chose Mass Communication at Ngee Ann Polytechnic, initially
          thinking I would become a journalist or filmmaker. What stayed with me was how much I enjoy
          learning about people and the human experience, along with a knack for making things look
          and feel right.
        </p>
        <p>
          Whether I’m working with makeup on my face, paint on a canvas or a web app on screen, I enjoy
          searching for the balance between what stakeholders want, what users need and what makes an
          experience feel intuitive and easy.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="about-tile about-tile--rule about-tile--dark">
        <p class="about-tile__number">02 / My favourite UI/UX rule</p>
        <h3>Good UI gets out of the way.</h3>
        <p>
          You know an interface is good when you do not notice it. Most people only notice UI when it
          is bad enough to get in their way.
        </p>

          Polytechnic.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="about-tile about-tile--nus">

        <p>
          I never expected to find myself studying Computer Science at NUS, but I did it! I came here
          because I wanted to understand how ideas are technically implemented, and because I have
          always thought nerdy things were cool.
        </p>
        <p>
          Tech feels like an entirely new domain to explore and unlock. The field switch has come with

          I meet. My curiosity and effort may not always be accurately reflected on my report card

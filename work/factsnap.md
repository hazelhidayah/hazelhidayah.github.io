problem: Questionable content spreads quickly, while fact-checking and cyber-awareness resources can feel disconnected from the moment when a user needs them.
process: I created the frontend wireframes and implemented the UI for FactSnap’s main forum page. The wider app lets users upload or photograph questionable content for AI-assisted analysis using reliable fact-checking sources, while quizzes, real-life scenarios and a leaderboard reinforce critical thinking and digital literacy through practice.
result: The project reached the final round of my first hackathon and became a working concept for learning how to recognise misinformation with friends.

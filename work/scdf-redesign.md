problem: The public-facing website needed a clearer information structure and a calmer, more accessible experience for people arriving with different needs and levels of urgency.
process: Over three months, I led the redesign as Creative Director. The team conducted interviews, synthesised pain points, reworked the information architecture and developed high-fidelity interactive prototypes. We also worked subtle motifs and crest-inspired details into the interface to reflect SCDF’s identity and symbolism.
result: The project produced an interactive website prototype that was presented to SCDF and GovTech.
